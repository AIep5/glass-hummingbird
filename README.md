A stained-glass hummingbird shattering mid-flight into a spiral flock of smaller luminous birds, jewel colours bursting against deep teal. The twist is the shatter itself: the bird does not break apart, it multiplies — one bold central shape dissolving into a swirling trail of glowing wings, so the eye is pulled along the spiral in a single rush. The saturated glass colours and the big, unmistakable bird shape still hit at 300 pixels across.
